# Theo Snyman

My personal site. [theosucksatcode.co.za](https://theosucksatcode.co.za)

## Why vanilla

No framework, no build step, no dependencies. Just HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

That's a deliberate choice, not a limitation. Those three were the first languages I ever learned, and most of my day to day work now runs through frameworks, tooling, and AI doing a lot of the heavy lifting. This site was an excuse to slow down and build something by hand again, the way I did when I was starting out. A portfolio felt like the right place for that: no reason to reach for anything heavier than what the page actually needs.

## What's here

- The homepage covers who I am and what I'm working on right now
- The CV page contains my full work history, education, and technical skills. Also downloadable as a PDF
- The technologies page contains the tools I actually reach for, split into what I use daily, what I build with, and what I'm currently picking up

## Stack

HTML, CSS, JavaScript. No frameworks, no package manager, no build step. Hosted on GitHub pages with a custom domain through Hostinger.
